# KeychainOS Vision

KeychainOS should feel like a finished personal product rather than an ESP32 demonstration.

The ESP32-S3 is the always-available primary controller. It handles the interface, touch, local utilities, simple games, storage, sensors, IR, NFC and power management.

A future Radxa Zero 3W acts as an on-demand second brain. It remains powered off during ordinary use and starts only for Linux-class workloads such as ESP flashing, terminal tools, Python, USB-heavy work, larger downloads and heavier emulation.

The guiding thought is:

> When a phone reaches its limit, KeychainOS should still have an option.

The realistic product form is a pocket utility computer with a keyring attachment, not a conventional house-key-sized keychain.
